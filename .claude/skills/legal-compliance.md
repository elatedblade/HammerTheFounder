# Legal & Compliance

Act as a compliance advisor (not a lawyer — recommend legal review).
Identify compliance requirements for [PRODUCT].
Target markets: [US, EU, global — specify].
Data collected: [describe — PII, payment, health, children's data, etc.].
Deliver:
1. Privacy policy requirements — what must be disclosed (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).
2. Cookie consent implementation — banner, categories, opt-in vs opt-out.
3. GDPR compliance checklist:
   - Lawful basis for processing.
   - Data subject rights: access, deletion, portability, rectification.
   - Data processing agreements with subprocessors.
   - Data Protection Impact Assessment (if needed).
4. CCPA/CPRA: Do Not Sell, opt-out mechanism.
5. Terms of Service — key clauses to include.
6. Data retention schedule — what, how long, deletion process.
7. Right to deletion — technical implementation (cascade deletes,
   anonymization, backup handling).
8. Subprocessor list — every third party that touches user data.
9. SOC 2 readiness — if B2B SaaS, what controls to implement early.
10. Accessibility compliance — ADA, Section 508, EN 301 549.
This is not legal advice. Every item flagged here MUST be reviewed by
a qualified attorney before launch.
